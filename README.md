Task Nook is your personal corner for keeping track of things that matter. Create different 'nooks' for work, home, hobbies, or whatever you need. Just describe what you need to do and let on-device AI turn it into a task - or let it automatically organize everything into categories for you. Check things off as you go. Everything stays on your device - we don't have servers, we don't collect data, and we definitely don't sell your stuff to advertisers. It's just a simple app made by indie devs who believe your notes should stay yours.

## Features

* Works 100% offline - no internet required, ever
* Your data never leaves your device (seriously, we don't even have servers)
* AI task creation - just describe what you need and let AI fill in the details
* Smart Categories - on-device AI that automatically groups your tasks for you
* Create multiple 'nooks' for different parts of your life
* Color-code your nooks however you like
* Set priorities on items so the important stuff floats to the top
* Export your data anytime - it's yours, take it wherever
* No subscriptions, no login, no nonsense
* Built by indie devs who actually care about privacy
